06The information desk

A few helpful answers.

Who is the association for?

Every Flushing High School graduate, from every graduating class. This is a place to reconnect, find alumni events, and discover ways to support the community.

Are the listed organizations association partners?

The Community Connections section is reserved for local outreach. Contacts and confirmed relationships will be added as they are verified.

How can I suggest a fundraiser or community opportunity?

Association contact details will appear in the Support section when confirmed. Fundraisers and outreach opportunities will be reviewed before they are listed.

Who can attend the reunion?

FHS graduates of ALL AGES and ALL GRADUATING YEARS are welcome at this reunion. There is no graduation-year cutoff. Guest arrangements will be announced with registration details.

When and where is the reunion?

The reunion is planned for June during Father’s Day weekend. The exact date, year, start time, and venue will be announced here.

What’s included?

A buffet luncheon, speakers, music, and time to reconnect with fellow alumni. The final itinerary and ticket details are coming soon.

Can I register and pay online?

V1 lets you try the registration steps. Live registration and secure online payment will open after the event details and ticket price are confirmed.

How do I share photos?

Select “Share an alumni photo,” choose a picture, and add a caption. Each photo must be approved individually. In this preview, photos stay in this browser tab.

What if I need accessibility or dietary accommodations?

Add your needs in the registration form. The organizers will provide venue accessibility and buffet information before live registration opens.
