A few helpful answers.

Who can attend?

All Flushing High School graduates are welcome, from every graduating class. Guest arrangements will be announced with registration details.

When and where is the reunion?

The reunion is planned for June during Father’s Day weekend. The exact date, year, start time, and venue will be announced here.

What’s included?

A buffet luncheon, speakers, music, and time to reconnect with fellow alumni. The final itinerary and ticket details are coming soon.

Can I register and pay online?

V1 lets you try the registration steps. Live registration and secure online payment will open after the event details and ticket price are confirmed.

How do I share photos?

Select “Share a reunion photo,” choose a picture, and add a caption. Each photo must be approved individually. In this preview, photos stay in this browser tab.

What if I need accessibility or dietary accommodations?

Add your needs in the registration form. The organizers will provide venue accessibility and buffet information before live registration opens.
